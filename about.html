Our story:

Light of Gospel Fellowship began in 1998 as a small prayer group meeting in a living room. As the group grew, members raised funds to build a church hall. Today about 300 people worship here each week.

Our Core Values

Worship
Honoring God through music, prayer, and Scripture.
Fellowship
Caring for one another through shared meals and support.
Service
Helping people through outreach and volunteering.
Growth
Learning through Bible study and mentoring.
